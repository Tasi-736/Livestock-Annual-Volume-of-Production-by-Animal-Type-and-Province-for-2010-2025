Livestock Production Analytics and Classification, 2010-2025

This project studies the annual volume of livestock production (carabao, cattle, goat, and hog) by province in the Philippines from 2010 to 2025. It shows the trends, compares animal types, and tests four machine learning models that classify a province’s production as high or low, then predicts the 2026 category.

Open the live app

What the app does

  • Data visualization: trends, scatterplot, and correlation heatmap
  • Descriptive analytics with bar charts, pie charts, and a boxplot
  • Four classification models: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting
  • If a model scores below 80% on validation, synthetic training records are tested and kept only when they help
  • ROC curves and confusion matrices for every model
  • A comparison table of accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC
  • High or low production predictions for 2026
